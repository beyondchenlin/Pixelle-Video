## Style Prompt
Build this like a premium launch film for a design-forward hardware product. The feeling should be warm, precise, and expensive: dark lacquer surfaces, copper light, editorial serif headlines, and restrained interface details. Motion should feel confident rather than flashy, with large shapes gliding in, soft lens-like blurs on scene changes, and a final lockup that feels like a closing keynote slide.

## Colors
- `#120E0B` background canvas
- `#241A14` secondary panel and shadow layer
- `#D8874F` copper accent
- `#F5EBDD` primary text
- `#A88D74` muted labels and dividers

## Typography
- `Playfair Display` for large editorial statements
- `Oswald` for labels, buttons, and UI details
- `JetBrains Mono` for specs and numeric values

## Motion
- Scene entrances should use blur-crossfade or gentle push-slide transitions
- Headlines move less than cards; cards and meters can travel farther
- Ambient background motion should be subtle and slow, never noisy

## What NOT to Do
- Do not use neon tech blues or glossy cyberpunk styling
- Do not use thin gray copy on the dark background
- Do not overfill the frame with too many feature bullets
- Do not make the timing feel bouncy or playful
