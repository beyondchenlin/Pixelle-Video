## Style Prompt
Make this feel like a high-confidence vertical social ad that was art-directed rather than auto-generated. The canvas should be dark and punchy with acid lime, coral, and off-white accents. Typography should feel poster-like and immediate, with oversized stacked words, short caption chips, and bold pacing. Motion should hit quickly, using energetic push-ins and chunky caption reveals, but it should still feel clean enough to read on a phone.

## Colors
- `#0C1016` background canvas
- `#A8FF60` acid lime accent
- `#FF7A59` coral accent
- `#F6F1E8` primary text
- `#1B222D` card surface

## Typography
- `Archivo Black` for big hooks and CTA lines
- `JetBrains Mono` for tags, labels, and counters

## Motion
- Use fast push-ins, caption pops, and small rotational offsets
- Keep scene transitions snappy and vertical-friendly
- Accent blocks can overshoot slightly, but long text should stay stable

## What NOT to Do
- Do not make it feel like a generic meme template
- Do not use pastel gradients or soft luxury pacing
- Do not shrink the text too much for the phone canvas
- Do not stack too many small elements on screen at once
