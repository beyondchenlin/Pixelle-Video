## Style Prompt
Design this like a technical keynote slide deck turned into motion graphics. The composition should feel precise, literate, and architectural: blueprint grids, disciplined spacing, bright cyan routing lines, and a calm dark canvas with one warm paper accent. It should read like someone explaining a codebase with confidence, not like a flashy product ad.

## Colors
- `#08131D` background canvas
- `#123040` panel surface
- `#7EE6FF` primary signal accent
- `#F1E9D7` warm highlight text
- `#9CB8C7` secondary labels and grid lines

## Typography
- `Oswald` for headlines and key labels
- `JetBrains Mono` for paths, package names, and numbers

## Motion
- Use push-slide and grid-dissolve style entrances
- Architecture lines should reveal directionally, not pop in
- Data cards should feel deliberate and modular, never elastic

## What NOT to Do
- Do not make it look like a hacker movie terminal
- Do not flood the frame with glowing neon effects
- Do not use playful bounce eases or emoji-like iconography
- Do not turn the repo explanation into a generic SaaS dashboard
